## v5.0 theme
From additive polish on a fixed architecture (v4.x) → a **topology change**: a persistent cross-project substrate that makes the orchestration *self-improving* across projects, not just self-aware.

## Recommended sequencing (from the roadmap)
1. **F4 brainstorm → design → build** — critical path; everything downstream waits on the index.
2. **A5** once F4's index exists.
3. **C4** — independent; parallelizable.
4. **E4** — opportunistic.
5. **F2** — own track; go/no-go decision after F4.

**Minimum-viable v5.0 = F4 + A5** (the self-improving core). C4/E4 are parallel value; F2 may defer to v6.
